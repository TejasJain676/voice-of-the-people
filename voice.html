RTI Application

RTI empowers citizens to seek information from government authorities. Submit RTI applications online through official portals of Central or State governments.

After submitting the application and paying the ₹10 fee, you receive a registration number which can be used to track the status. Authorities must reply within 30 days.

RTI ensures transparency, accountability, and citizen participation in governance.
