Raise Your Voice

Report verified civic issues and receive a ready-to-file official complaint draft.

Citizens can use this platform to report genuine local civic issues such as malfunctioning street lights, damaged roads and potholes, improper garbage disposal, water supply problems, drainage and sewage issues, public safety hazards, and other community-related concerns. By submitting verified details along with photographic evidence, users receive a structured, ready-to-file complaint draft in PDF format. This system simplifies the complaint process, reduces confusion about formal reporting, and empowers citizens to approach official government portals with clarity and confidence. Ultimately, it encourages responsible civic participation, improves transparency, and helps bridge the gap between public grievances and administrative action.
